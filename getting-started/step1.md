# Getting Started

Welcome to the **Barclays Payments Data Product Hands-on Lab**.

In this lab you'll build an AI-enriched data product on Snowflake in ~90 minutes, using Cortex Code, dbt, Cortex AI, Dynamic Tables, Semantic Views, and Snowflake CoWork.

---

## Step 1: Register & Open the Lab Guide

### 1. Register for your Snowflake account

A dedicated Snowflake account has been provisioned for this lab. Click below to register — you'll receive credentials within a few minutes.

**[Register for your account →](https://go.dataops.live/tech-fest-barclays/register)**

### 2. Open the interactive lab guide

Once you have your credentials, open the full lab guide in your browser. This is where all the step-by-step instructions, SQL code, and progress tracking live:

**[Open Lab Guide →](https://gemmamcginlay-sf.github.io/barclays-hol/barclays_hol_data_product.html)**

### 3. Follow the steps in order

The lab guide has 6 steps. Work through them sequentially — each step builds on the previous one. You'll copy SQL directly from the guide into Snowsight.

---

## What you'll need

- A modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, or Edge)
- The Snowflake credentials from Step 1 above

## What you'll build

| Step | Topic | Time |
|------|-------|------|
| 1 | Environment Setup | ~5 min |
| 2 | Load Payments Data | ~10 min |
| 3 | dbt with Cortex Code | ~20 min |
| 4 | Cortex AI + Dynamic Tables | ~20 min |
| 5 | Snowflake CoWork (Cortex Analyst) | ~15 min |
| 6 | Document & Describe | ~10 min |
