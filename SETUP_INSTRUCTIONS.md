# Barclays Hands-On Lab: Setup Instructions

## Account Registration

1. Go to: **https://go.dataops.live/tech-fest-barclays/register**
2. **Scan the QR code** on a personal phone
3. **Register with a personal email** (not Barclays corporate email, since those are being blocked)
4. You will receive the password to your personal email
5. **Login on your phone** with your personal email + the new password

## Access Snowflake

Open the Snowflake account URL on the **Barclays VDI**:

```
https://sfsehol-tech_fest_barclays_xxxxxx.snowflakecomputing.com/console/login
```

> Replace `xxxxxx` with your unique code from the registration link.
